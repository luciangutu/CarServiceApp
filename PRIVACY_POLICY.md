# Privacy Policy for CarServiceApp

**Last Updated:** October 12, 2026

## Overview

CarServiceApp ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Data Collection

CarServiceApp does not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers. All information you enter into the App remains exclusively on your device.

### Data Stored Locally
- **Vehicle Information:** Make, model, year, license plate, VIN, and notes
- **Service Records:** Dates, kilometers, service names, operations performed, and costs
- **Maintenance Intervals:** Component-specific maintenance schedules
- **Operation Suggestions:** Reusable service operation templates

This data is stored in a local SQLite database on your Android device only.

## 2. No Data Collection

- We do not collect usage data
- We do not collect analytics
- We do not collect crash reports
- We do not collect device information
- We do not transmit any information to external servers
- We do not share data with third parties

## 3. Data Storage

All user data is stored in a local SQLite database on your Android device. Data never leaves your device except when you explicitly choose to export a backup file.

### Backups
When you export your data, the App generates a JSON file on your device. You control where this file goes (Google Drive, email, cloud storage, etc.). We have no access to exported backups.

## 4. Third-Party Services

CarServiceApp does not use any analytics, advertising, or third-party data collection services. The only external interaction occurs when you manually export a backup file through Android's native file sharing — which you completely control.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. No data leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

## 6. User Rights

You have full control over your data:
- **Access:** You can view all your data within the App at any time
- **Export:** You can export your complete database as a JSON file
- **Deletion:** You can delete any vehicle, service record, or maintenance interval directly in the App. Uninstalling the App removes all local data.
- **Portability:** Your exported JSON files are portable and not locked to any proprietary format

## 7. Children's Privacy

CarServiceApp is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it immediately.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the App following changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
